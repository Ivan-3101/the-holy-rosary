How to Pray the Rosary

The Rosary is a prayer of the heart: the lips pray the Our Father, Hail Mary and Glory Be, while the mind contemplates the mysteries of Christ’s life with Mary.

  1. The Sign of the Cross & the Creed

    Holding the crucifix, make the Sign of the Cross and pray the Apostles’ Creed.

    On the crucifix

  2. Our Father

    Pray the Our Father on the first large bead.

    First large bead

  3. Three Hail Marys

    Pray a Hail Mary on each of the next three beads, for an increase of faith, hope and charity.

    Three small beads

  4. Glory Be

    Pray the Glory Be.

    On the chain

  5. Announce the First Mystery, then the Our Father

    Announce the first mystery (for example, “The First Joyful Mystery: The Annunciation”) and pray the Our Father.

    Large bead below the centerpiece

  6. Ten Hail Marys

    Pray ten Hail Marys, one on each bead of the decade, while meditating on the mystery.

    Ten small beads

  7. Glory Be & O My Jesus

    Pray the Glory Be, then the Fatima Prayer: “O my Jesus, forgive us our sins…”

    On the chain

  8. The Next Four Decades

    On each following large bead, announce the next mystery and pray the Our Father; then continue as before until all five decades are prayed.

    Around the loop

  9. Hail, Holy Queen & the Concluding Prayers

    After the fifth decade, pray the Hail, Holy Queen and the concluding prayer. Many then pray the Litany of Loreto, the Prayer to St. Michael, and other prayers.

    On the centerpiece

  10. The Sign of the Cross

    End as you began, with the Sign of the Cross.

Which Mysteries Today?

MysteriesPrayed on
The Joyful MysteriesMonday & Saturday
The Luminous MysteriesThursday
The Sorrowful MysteriesTuesday & Friday
The Glorious MysteriesWednesday & Sunday

This weekly pattern was proposed by Pope St. John Paul II in Rosarium Virginis Mariae (2002), the letter that also added the Luminous Mysteries. It is a guide, not a rule: any set of mysteries may be prayed on any day.

Praying together

When the Rosary is prayed in a group, a leader announces each mystery and prays the first half of each prayer; everyone answers with the second half. On this site, the responses are marked with a gold line.

“The family that prays together stays together.” Venerable Patrick Peyton

Meditate, don’t rush

The Hail Marys give the Rosary its gentle rhythm, but its heart is the mystery. Before each decade, picture the scene from the Gospel, and let the words carry you while you stay with Jesus and Mary.

Indulgences

The Church grants a plenary indulgence, under the usual conditions (sacramental confession, Holy Communion, prayer for the intentions of the Pope, and freedom from all attachment to sin), to those who devoutly pray five decades of the Rosary without interruption in a church or oratory, or together as a family, a religious community or a pious association. In other circumstances a partial indulgence is granted.
